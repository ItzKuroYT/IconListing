About this server

Installation Guide (for Modpack) - You can download the Modpack directly from our website and then just double click install! DM me on Discord if you need help with installation (ogsigurd) We are essentially a super-upgraded "Factions" type server. We use a custom plugin called "Feudal" that allows for: Only online wars War and Economy Points (so military and economic conquest are viable) Advanced mechanics like: Tribute, Taxes, and custom inter-Kingdom politics. We are "vanilla-esque" but are slightly in the future compared to vanilla (welcome to the age of early firearms, pikes, and simple flying machines!)
