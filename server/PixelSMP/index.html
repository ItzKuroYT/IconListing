About this server

Description supplied by the server owner.

Welcome to PixelSMP, a Minecraft Survival Multiplayer server combining the competitive gameplay of Lifesteal with an enchantment-focused survival experience! Whether you enjoy PvP, collecting powerful gear, building, grinding, trading, exploring, or competing with other players, PixelSMP offers plenty of opportunities to develop your equipment, strengthen your abilities, and make your mark on the server. PixelSMP combines two exciting gameplay styles where survival, progression, and player interaction are at the center of the experience. Fight other players, take risks, improve your gear through enchantments, and work toward becoming one of the strongest players on the server. Whether you prefer preparing for intense PvP encounters, gathering resources, establishing a base, or developing your equipment, there is always something to work toward. With a smaller world border, players are kept closer together, encouraging encounters, rivalries, team-ups, trading, and competition. You won't have to travel thousands of blocks just to find another player, making the world feel more connected and creating more opportunities for interaction. We also offer a variety of quality-of-life plugins designed to improve the survival experience without taking away from the core Minecraft gameplay. Tired of spending ages collecting string? SlashString provides free string through a convenient command, with a cooldown to keep things balanced. This is especially useful for players building farms and other resource-heavy setups. Villager management is also made easier with ClickVillagers, allowing players to pick up villagers instead of dealing with complicated transportation methods. We also have InstantRestock, which allows villagers to restock their trades instantly, saving time and making trading more convenient. Looking for a better way to communicate with other players? PixelSMP also has Simple Voice Chat installed! Talk to nearby players using proximity voice chat, coordinate with your teammates, communicate during PvP, or simply socialize while exploring and building together. Voice chat adds another layer of interaction and makes playing with friends even more enjoyable. Players will need the compatible Simple Voice Chat mod installed to use this feature. Additional features include convenient player commands, teleport requests, gameplay improvements, and more quality-of-life additions to make your experience smoother. PixelSMP is built around its community. Team up with friends, make enemies, establish rivalries, trade resources, create impressive builds, improve your equipment, and compete to become the strongest player. Whether you're a dedicated PvP player or someone who prefers the survival and progression side of Minecraft, there is a place for you here. Join PixelSMP, strengthen your gear, find your allies, and see how far you can climb!
